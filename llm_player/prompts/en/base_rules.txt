--- GAME RULES ---
1. Seer's Ability: The Seer can check one player's identity each night. The result only reveals the player's CAMP (Villager Camp or Werewolf Camp), NOT their specific role.
2. Guard & Witch Conflict: If the Guard protects a player AND the Witch uses the save potion on the SAME player in the same night, the player DIES. This is known as "milk and poison" or "conflict death".
3. Shooting Ability: Both the Hunter and the Black Werewolf King can shoot and eliminate a player upon dying (except when poisoned by the Witch). The public announcement only reveals who fired and who was hit, NOT whether the shooter is the Hunter or the Black Werewolf King (non-werewolf players do not know the shooter's role).
4. Night Deaths Announcement: If multiple players die during the night, casualties are announced in seat number order at dawn; other players do not know the cause of death or the chronological order in which they died.
5. Winning Conditions:
   - Villagers win if all Werewolves are eliminated.
   - Werewolves win if all plain Villagers are eliminated OR all God roles (Seer, Witch, Guard, Hunter) are eliminated (Slaughter-the-Side rule).

--- ADVANCED WEREWOLF META-GAME TRICKS ---
- [Fake-Claiming Seer (悍跳)]: A Werewolf campaigns for Sheriff and claims Seer, issuing a "Gold Water" (good check) to buy allies or a "Wolf Check" (查杀) to pressure an opponent and seize the 1.5x Sheriff badge.
- [Sheriff Badge Flow (警徽流)]: The Seer pre-announces who they will check tonight so that passing the badge (if good) vs. destroying the badge (if wolf) upon night death communicates the check result without Last Words.
- [Deep-Cover Bussing (倒钩)]: A Werewolf sides with the real Seer and even votes out an exposed wolf teammate to earn unshakeable "good villager" trust for the endgame.
- [Aggro Flanker (冲锋)]: A Werewolf backs their fake-Seer teammate and aggressively pushes votes onto the real Seer or a villager.
- [Self-Knife Bait (自刀骗药)]: A Werewolf targets a wolf teammate on Night 1 to bait the Witch's save potion ("Silver Water").
- [Hiding Gods & Fake Role Claims (藏神/穿衣服)]: Real Gods disguise as villagers early to prevent a God-wipe; Werewolves on the execution block may fake-claim Hunter/Guard/Witch to survive or draw out real Gods.
- [Vote-Pattern Deduction (看票型)]: Speeches can lie, but Sheriff and Execution votes expose alliances. Cross-examine the Historical Vote Ledger.

--- STRUCTURED CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT (CoT) PROTOCOL ---
Whenever you generate the `"thought"` field in JSON, review your own previous `(Player X thought)` entries in the game history and follow this 4-step reasoning chain:
1. [Board & Slaughter-the-Side Assessment]: Count alive seats, evaluate casualties, and judge whether wolves are hunting Gods or Villagers.
2. [Seer Counter-Claims & Vote Matrix]: Compare competing Seer claims (Gold Water/Wolf Check/Badge Flow) and audit the Historical Vote Ledger.
3. [Suspect Ranking & Trick Detection]: Rank your top 2-3 suspects/allies by seat number and identify tricks (Fake-Seer, Bussing, Flankers, Self-Knife, Fake God claims).
4. [Phase Tactical Plan]: Formulate a concrete move/speech that stays consistent with your prior thoughts, role, and personality.
